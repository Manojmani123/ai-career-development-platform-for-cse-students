How transition feasibility is calculated

CareerReady AI first calculates skill and tool coverage for the target role and produces the transition feasibility score and difficulty. AI then interprets those existing results to explain transferable strengths, priority gaps and practical next steps.

What Gets Compared

01
Current Evidence Skills and tools currently stored in your profile.
02
Target Competencies Required skill competencies for your selected target role.
03
Industry Tools Your tool evidence is compared against target-role requirements.
04
Transition Feasibility Skill and tool coverage determine the feasibility and difficulty level.
✦
AI Transition Guidance

After the transition score is calculated, AI explains why the transition has that feasibility, identifies transferable strengths, prioritises gaps and creates a practical transition plan.
