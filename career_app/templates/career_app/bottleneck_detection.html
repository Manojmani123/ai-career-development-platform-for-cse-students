How the result is determined

CareerReady AI first evaluates your existing readiness and practical project evidence to identify the primary bottleneck. AI then interprets that diagnosis and provides prioritised improvement guidance without replacing the original detected bottleneck.

What Gets Analysed

01
Readiness Scores Academic, industry and overall readiness evidence.

04
Project Evidence How strongly your projects demonstrate target-role skills and tools.
